# Provider marks

Brand silhouettes are displayed in a shared monochrome khaki-on-stone badge system. OpenAI Images shares the OpenAI identity. Custom providers use the generic catalog symbol.

- Codex, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini: @lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.95.1, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons (MIT).
- GitHub, Railway, Vercel, Linear, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Stripe and Notion: Simple Icons 16.32.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0).
- Canva and Slack: Simple Icons 14.15.0 (CC0).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. No endorsement is implied. SVG geometry is retained; color, sizing and surrounding badges match Relay's interface.
